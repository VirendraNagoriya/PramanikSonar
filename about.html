ABOUT US

Dada Gujar – Founder of Pramanik Sonar

From earning just ₹5 a week in 1974 to building C’Lai World, a trusted name in men’s fashion, Dada Gujar’s journey is a story of grit and dreams.

What began with a small loan of ₹750 and a stitching machine has now evolved into his lifelong passion — gold. For him, gold is not just metal; it is purity, celebration, and emotion that connects generations.

With Pramanik Sonar, he brings jewellery that blends authentic tradition with modern elegance — from everyday treasures to timeless pieces for life’s grand moments.

His belief is simple: businesses are built on hard work, but legacies are built on passion. And his legacy now shines in gold.
