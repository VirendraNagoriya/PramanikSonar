What is HUID? Gold Purity & HUID Explained

HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) is a six-digit alphanumeric code assigned to every hallmarked gold jewellery item in India. This ensures authenticity and traceability, and is verifiable through the BIS CARE app.

In this article, we explain how to check HUID, its benefits, and how it protects customers while purchasing gold jewellery.

Benefits of HUID:

1.Consumer Protection: Helps consumers identify and avoid fake or substandard products. 2.Transparency: Ensures transparency in the jewelry industry. 3.Traceability: Allows for easy tracing of the jewelry back to its jeweller and the hallmarking center. 4.Consumer Rights: Protects consumer rights by enabling them to lodge complaints or grievances online. 5.Resale Value: The presence of HUID can positively impact the resale value of the jewelry.

Understanding Gold Hallmarks

Gold hallmarks are essential for verifying the purity and authenticity of gold items, typically represented by numbers and symbols.

Purity Levels

  • 375: 37.5% gold (9K)
  • 585: 58.5% gold (14K)
  • 750: 75% gold (18K)
  • 916: 91.6% gold (22K)
  • 990: 99% gold (23.75K)
  • 999: 99.9% gold (24K, pure gold)

Components of Hallmarks

  • Assay Office Mark: Indicates where the gold was tested for purity.
  • Purity Mark: Shows the gold content in karats or as a decimal.
  • Date Letter: In some countries, a letter may indicate the year of hallmarking.
  • Maker's Mark: Identifies the manufacturer or jeweler responsible for the item.

Importance of Hallmarking

Hallmarking ensures that consumers receive genuine gold products that meet specific purity standards. It protects buyers from counterfeit items and provides assurance regarding the quality of their purchases.

Conclusion:

While this overview does not provide a specific chart, it summarizes the key aspects of gold hallmarks, including purity levels and the significance of various marks. For a detailed chart, you may want to refer to specific jewelry or gold trading websites that specialize in hallmarking standards. Understanding these hallmarks is crucial for anyone looking to buy or sell gold jewellery, ensuring they make informed decisions based on the quality of the gold.
